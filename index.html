About Me

My journey into technology began with design and an ambition to create meaningful value through innovation.

After graduating from university, where I focused primarily on graphic design and branding, I joined a startup company in Nagoya, Japan. Working in a dynamic startup environment gave me the opportunity to experience the entrepreneurial mindset firsthand and gain exposure to the startup ecosystem, business development, and cross-industry collaboration.

Through my work, I came to appreciate how ideas, technology, and people can come together to create new opportunities. This experience gradually shaped my ambition to challenge myself beyond Japan and become part of the North American technology market while I was still in my twenties.

With that goal in mind, I made the decision to move to Vancouver, Canada, on my own to pursue further studies in full-stack web development.

Today, I am building on my creative background by developing technical skills in front-end and back-end development. I believe that my foundation in branding, visual communication, and design, combined with my growing technical expertise and startup experience, allows me to approach challenges from both creative and practical perspectives.

My goal is to bridge design, business, and technology — contributing to innovative products, meaningful digital experiences, and ambitious teams within the tech industry.
